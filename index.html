What's the difference between borrowing on Blend and margin borrowing on Vanna?
{{ s.label }} {{ s.tool }}

Blend is an isolated pool: you post collateral there and borrow against it at that pool's rate, and the position stays inside the pool. Vanna margin borrows against your whole account, so the same collateral can back a Blend supply, a Soroswap LP and an open position at once.

NoteShared collateral means one liquidation can unwind more than one position. Rates move with pool utilisation.
